Our Philosophy

A single herb garden, a lifetime of restraint.

Chef Alexander Thorne opened Basilico with one rule: nothing reaches the plate that hasn't earned its place. Every menu begins in the rooftop herb garden, where basil is grown in eleven varieties and used the way other kitchens reserve for truffle — sparingly, and always at the center of the dish.

Trained across three Michelin kitchens before returning home, he built Basilico's cellar one vintage at a time and still walks the dining room himself, most nights, before service begins.
